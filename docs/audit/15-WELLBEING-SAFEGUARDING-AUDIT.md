# 15 — Child Wellbeing & Safeguarding Audit

## Protocols Implemented
1. **Soothing Voice Escalation**: When distress is detected, the AI companion does NOT mute; it speaks calming reassurance, provides crisis numbers, and alerts the caregiver.
2. **Caregiver Harm Exception (CPWB 1121)**: If a child discloses abuse by a parent or guardian, parent notification is actively suppressed to prevent retaliation, and the incident is escalated to Child Protection authorities.\n
