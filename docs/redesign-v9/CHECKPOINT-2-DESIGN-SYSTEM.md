# WEEBLY V9 — CHECKPOINT 2: INTERNATIONAL BRAND, DESIGN SYSTEM & THREE HOMEPAGE CONCEPTS

## 1. Brand Positioning & Visual Identity

**Brand Essence:**
> *"A calm, intelligent, joyful world where children talk, learn, create, play and grow — supported by trusted adults."*

### Dual-Environment Architecture
Weebly V9 establishes a unified design language split into two calibrated environmental modes:
1. **Child Magic World (`/youth/*`, `/creative-studio`):**
   - Warm ivory (`#FAF7F2`) and soft sky (`#E0F2FE`) surfaces.
   - Oversized 64px–88px touch targets, custom SVG companion portraits (`Kai`, `Mia`, `Lumi`, `Zayn`), audio-first prompts, and maximum 3–4 choices per screen for early learners.
2. **Adult Operational Studio (`/parent`, `/teacher/dashboard`, `/school/portal`, `/professional/portal`, `/admin/*`):**
   - Crisp off-white (`#FAF7F2` / `#FFFFFF`) with deep ink (`#0F172A`) typography, structured tables, skill mastery badges, and non-clinical developmental observations.
